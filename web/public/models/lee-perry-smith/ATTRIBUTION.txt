This asset is the licensed head study used for the cinematic guide's face.
It is a static scan: the prototype clips the original bust at the neck and does
not rig or animate facial expressions. The hoodie, hands, body, walk cycle and
camera choreography are original Three.js geometry/code created for JobHunt Hub.

Attribution is also shown in the dashboard's "Contact / credits" card and in
docs/ARCHITECTURE.md and docs/IMPLEMENTATION.md.
