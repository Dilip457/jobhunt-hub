Student character asset
=======================

Model: "Casual Man" by Grab3D
Source: https://grab3d.com/models/characters/man/
License: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain)
License text: https://grab3d.com/license/

Downloaded: 2026-10-21

Modifications made for JobHunt India:
- Mesh simplified from 244,257 to 85,487 triangles with glTF Transform for web delivery.
- Textures resized to 1024px, encoded as WebP and embedded in the GLB; the loading poster
  (`portrait.webp`) is a render of the same modified asset.
- Basecolour atlas cleaned for the cinematic: the jacket/denim were recoloured to a flat
  navy fabric, the hair to a flat warm brown, and small bright/orange texture flecks were
  suppressed. The white knit top, skin, beard and shoe details are retained.
- Material switched to a uniform matte finish (metalness 0, roughness 0.84, no
  roughness/metallic maps, reduced normal strength) to remove specular fireflies.
- Non-essential runtime geometry (the earlier 3D glasses) removed so the face reads cleanly.
- A breathing idle clip generated from a neutral walk pose at runtime.
- The walk clip is speed-matched to the 1.8 m travel cycle so the feet do not slide.

The source asset is a stylized character, not a photoreal scan. Visually it remains a
bearded adult in a technical bomber jacket, not a hoodie. It has no facial blendshapes,
eye rig, or lip-sync animation. The walk and run clips are the only animation clips
shipped in the GLB. This limitation is documented in docs/IMPLEMENTATION.md and is not
disguised as a final facial-rigged asset.
