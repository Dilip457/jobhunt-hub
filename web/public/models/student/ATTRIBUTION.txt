Student character asset
=======================

Model: "Casual Man" by Grab3D
Source: https://grab3d.com/models/characters/man/
License: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain)
License text: https://grab3d.com/license/

Downloaded: 2026-10-21

Modifications made for JobHunt India:
- Mesh simplified from 244,257 to 85,487 triangles with glTF Transform for web delivery.
- Basecolour atlas embedded at 2048px; normal and metallic-roughness maps at 1024px, all
  encoded as WebP. `scene.webp` is a wide render of the modified asset used by the
  loading/static background; `portrait.webp` is a close-up reference render. Both were
  regenerated from the clean-clothing runtime on 2026-10-22. Neither is a raw source-photo
  crop.
- The source atlas has scrambled garment UV islands: jacket, knit top, hair and skin share
  texture space, so sampling it directly produced brown “tears” and dark flecks. The
  runtime now splits the body geometry into clean materials: a blue bomber, navy jeans, a
  white t-shirt and a feathered skin tint around the neck. The neck boundary is a
  per-vertex colour blend; the t-shirt panel and the collar skin band are masked per fragment
  in the body shader, from the bind-pose position, so their edges stay crisp instead of
  following the chest's coarse triangle size. The tee mask also runs on the jeans material,
  because the shirt hem drops below the jacket onto the front of the hips. Head, hair, beard,
  hands and white sneakers keep the original textured atlas.
- Textured material finish: `metalness: 0`, roughness `0.86`, normal scale `0.38`,
  environment intensity `0.42`. Clothing groups use matte `MeshStandardMaterial` with
  per-vertex colours and dithering.
- The atlas was also cleaned of bright/orange flecks; the embedded 2048px basecolour is a
  cleaned variant. Normal and roughness maps remain at 1024px. The cleaned atlas is sampled
  only by the head, hair, hands and shoe triangles; garment triangles use the flat
  materials, so residual garment-island artifacts in the atlas are not rendered.
- Non-essential runtime geometry (the earlier 3D glasses) removed so the face reads cleanly.
- A breathing idle clip generated from a neutral walk pose at runtime.
- The walk clip is speed-matched to the 1.8 m travel cycle so the feet do not slide.
- The stock walk plants the feet about 33 cm apart, which read as a waddle. The runtime
  adducts both upper-leg tracks by 10 degrees (about the forward axis) so the feet pass
  under the body about 12 cm apart, matching the reference footage, before the idle pose is
  sampled from the clip.

The source asset is a stylized character, not a photoreal scan. Visually it remains a
bearded adult in a technical bomber jacket, not a hoodie. It has no facial blendshapes,
eye rig, or lip-sync animation. The white t-shirt panel and neck tint are clean
material and shader treatments, not re-authored garment topology; the walk and run clips are
the only animation clips shipped in the GLB. This limitation is documented in
docs/IMPLEMENTATION.md and is not disguised as a final facial-rigged asset.
